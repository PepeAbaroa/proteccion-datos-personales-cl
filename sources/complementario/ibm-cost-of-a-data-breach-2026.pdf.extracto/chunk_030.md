Agent adoption plans increased in response 
to frontier AI model threats
Because of threats from frontier AI models, a majority of 
organizations (74%) have rethought if and where they deploy 
agents in their SOCs. In follow-on research, most organizations 
said they plan to deploy agents in areas where they were 
underrepresented, such as in alert triage (60% versus 34%), 
penetration testing (52% versus 33%), and vulnerability scans 
and management (37% versus 18%). See Figures 40 and 41.
