What is PII (Personally identifiable information)?
Personally identifiable information (PII) or personal data, is defined
 differently in various data protection laws, but generally, it include
s any information that can be used to identify a specific individual either 
directly or indirectly.
Organizations are required to create, implement, and maintain suitable
 controls to safeguard and manage individuals' privacy throughout th
e entire life cycle of personal and associated information. This includ
es identifying internal and external requirements that shape the 
organization's privacy program and practices.
