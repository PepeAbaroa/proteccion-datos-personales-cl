Here are several effective ways to communicate
privacy notices:
Website: Publish the privacy notice prominently on the
 organization's website. Provide a direct link to the notice fro
m relevant pages, such as the homepage, registration or sign-up 
pages, and any pages where personal data is collected.
Mobile Applications: Display the privacy notice within the mobile
 application, accessible through settings or 
     account management sections.
