2. External Requirement
M I S S I O N
Legal and Regulatory Compliance: Ensuring compliance with relevant privacy laws
 and regulations applicable to the organization's operations, such as GDPR, CCPA, or 
local data protection laws.
Third-Party Management: Implementing measures to ensure that third-party vendors
 and service providers who process personal data on behalf of the organization 
adhere to the same privacy standards and requirements.
Data Subject Rights: Providing mechanisms for individuals to exercise their rights
 regarding their personal data, such as access, rectification, erasure, and data 
portability.
Transparency and Accountability: Being transparent about data processing
 practices and accountable for compliance with privacy commitments made to 
individuals and regulatory authorities.
International Data Transfers: Ensuring that any transfer of personal data outside the
 organization's jurisdiction complies with legal requirements for international data 
transfers, such as implementing appropriate safeguards under GDPR.
