Communication of Privacy notice
Privacy notices should be communicated in a clear, accessible, and
 understandable manner to ensure individuals are informed abou
t their privacy rights and how their personal data is being handled.
Example: 
Signs on buildings or interior walls that inform individuals that
 closed-circuit television (CCTV) cameras are in use can indee
d function as privacy notices. They serve to inform individuals abo
ut the presence of surveillance and the collection of their personal 
data through video recordings.
