5. Third-Party Management: 
Evaluate controls over third-party service providers and vendors that
process personal data on behalf of the organization. This includes
contractual agreements, security assessments, and monitoring of third-
party compliance with data protection obligations.
6. Data Retention and Disposal: 
Assess policies and procedures for retaining personal data only for as
long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, and
securely disposing of data that is no longer needed.
7. Cross-Border Data Regulations
Auditors ensure that cross-border data transfers comply with
international privacy laws. For example, they verify compliance with
GDPR when data is transferred between the EU and the US.
