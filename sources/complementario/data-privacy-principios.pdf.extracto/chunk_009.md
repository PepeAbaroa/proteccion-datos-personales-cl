A privacy notice is an outward-facing document that describes an
 organization's data handling practices to individuals whose persona
l data is being collected. It serves as a key transparency mechanis
m, providing clear and understandable information about how 
the organization processes, uses, stores, and protects personal data. 
Key elements typically included in a privacy notice are:
Data Controller Identification: Information about the organization 
collecting the data and their contact details.
Purposes of Data Processing: Clear explanations of why 
personal data is being collected and how it will be used.
