Email: Send a concise summary or link to the full privacy notice in
 emails where personal data is collected, such as newsletters, 
promotional emails, or registration confirmations.
Paper Forms: Include a summary or full privacy notice on paper
 forms where personal data is collected, ensuring it is easily 
readable.
In-Person: For organizations interacting with individuals face-to-
face, provide printed copies of the privacy notice or direct them 
to where they can access it online.
Privacy Dashboard: Create a dedicated privacy dashboard within
 user accounts where individuals can access and 
     manage their privacy settings and view the 
      privacy notice.
