Consent form is Used to document the agreement of 
     data subjects with privacy terms. 
     Example
     Before participating in an online survey, respondents are required to
     check a box indicating their consent to the collection and use of their
     responses for research purposes, with a clear explanation of ho 
     their data will be used and protected.
A consent form could be a button you click to agree or disagree, a
 box you check on a website below a privacy notice, or a place where 
you sign on paper forms that have similar privacy information.
Data subjects have the right to withdraw their consent at any time,
 and mechanisms used to capture consent should take into accoun
t the potential for a data subject to do so.
