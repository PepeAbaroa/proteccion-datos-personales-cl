8. Access Controls: 
Evaluate controls that restrict access to personal data based on roles
and responsibilities, ensuring only authorized individuals have access.
This includes authentication mechanisms, user access reviews, and
segregation of duties.
9. Data Encryption: 
Assess encryption methods used to protect sensitive personal data
both at rest (stored data) and in transit (data being transmitted),
ensuring encryption standards are in line with best practices and
regulatory requirements.
