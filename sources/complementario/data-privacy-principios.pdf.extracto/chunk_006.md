Internal Requirement
1.
M I S S I O N
Policy and Procedures: Developing and implementing privacy policies and
 procedures that outline how personal data will be collected, processed, stored, and 
protected within the organization.
Data Inventory and Mapping: Conducting a thorough inventory of personal data
 collected and processed by the organization, and mapping its flow through different 
systems and processes.
Risk Assessment and Management: Conducting privacy impact assessments (PIAs)
 and implementing risk management practices to identify and mitigate privacy risks 
associated with data processing activities.
Training and Awareness: Providing regular training and awareness programs for
 employees to ensure they understand their responsibilities regarding data protection 
and privacy.
Access Controls: Implementing technical and organizational measures to restrict 
access to personal data to authorized personnel only.
Incident Response: Establishing procedures for responding to and reporting data 
breaches or privacy incidents in a timely manner.
